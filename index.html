Cyber Safety Tips

01. Use a strong and unique password for every important account.
02. Never share your OTP, PIN or password with anyone.
03. Do not click suspicious links received through unknown messages.
04. Enable Two-Factor Authentication whenever possible.
05. Keep your operating system and applications updated.
06. Avoid downloading files from unknown or untrusted websites.
